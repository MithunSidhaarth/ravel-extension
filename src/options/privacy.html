RAVEL
PRIVACY POLICY

The short version

Ravel does not send your browsing activity anywhere. There is no account, no server, and no third party involved at any point. Everything described below happens only inside your own browser, on your own device.

What Ravel stores

While you browse, Ravel keeps a local record of page titles, URLs, and timestamps for the tabs it's allowed to see, so it can group them into threads and let you search for them later. This is written to this browser's local IndexedDB only. It is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted over the network.

Why Ravel asks for its permissions

"tabs" lets Ravel see the titles and URLs of your open tabs, which is how it builds threads. "alarms" schedules the local cleanup that prunes old activity past your retention setting. The content script that runs on http/https pages reads signals already visible on the page (its title, a bit of structure) to help identify what it is. None of it leaves the browser.

How long it's kept

You choose the retention window in Settings, from 30 days up to forever. "Delete everything" in Settings wipes all local data immediately, and uninstalling the extension removes it too.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version replaces this page. There's nothing to opt into or out of beyond what's already in Settings.
