COOKIE POLICY

Ravel doesn't use cookies.

It doesn't set them, read them, or rely on any site's cookies to work. There's no tracking pixel, no analytics beacon, and no third-party script anywhere in the extension.

What it uses instead

The only storage Ravel touches is its own local IndexedDB database, separate from any site's cookies or storage, and never shared with the pages you visit. See the Privacy Policy for what's kept there.
