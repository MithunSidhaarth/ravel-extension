RAVEL
ABOUT

Close a tab without losing your place.

Ravel watches the tabs you actually use, groups them into threads by topic, and lets you close the stale ones in one motion. Nothing is thrown away when you do: every tab it held is kept as a spool, a digest you can search and reopen later.

Local-only, by design

Ravel has no account, no server, and no sync. Everything it remembers lives in this browser's local storage, on this device, for as long as you keep it. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

Three moves

Track: Ravel follows what you open, quietly, in the background. Ravel it: when a thread's gone quiet, close it in one motion and keep the digest. Recall: half remember something later? Describe it in Search and Ravel traces back how you got there.
